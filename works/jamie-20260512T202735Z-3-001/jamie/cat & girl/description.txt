cat & girl calendar 2025 shows the slice-of-life mundane beauty every year brings. done digitally and made with love.

poem written through the months:

i still have bite marks from last year's winter
thawing out, dripping mug-ring stains onto wooden floors
my mother's birthday, i help her blow out the candles
she lets me
and again, an old friend is back
i belong here, in June
making something to mourn when the summer is done
so i hold the warmth tight inside my hand
and open it up again
it's still new, every time
i think i like it that way
it's been good, and so much better
