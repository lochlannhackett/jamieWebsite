'Dig Down' is a silent book depicting the story of a girl who gets lost in another world and has to deal with the consequences of choice. The book is ultimately about the comfort of depression, falling down a hole and having the make the active decision to climb out yourself. In the end, it doesn't matter if you have people reaching out a hand on the other side of you don't want to be in a back in the light. 'Dig Down' is a mixed media piece, using chalk pastels & colouring pencils combined with digital practice. made with love.
