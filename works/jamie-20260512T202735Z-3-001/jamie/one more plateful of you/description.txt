One more plateful of you

riso printed short narrative created for a collaborative book on the theme of Hunger. Digitally done and made with love.

isn't it sweet the way i want? stuck in your teeth and licked clean?
open my mouth a little wider and put hands on red rib cages
this is what it means to be hungry
one more prayer and one more push 
one more plateful of you
i could just stay like this
i could just swallow you whole
